ABOUT THE BUSINESS

GIS intelligence focused on changing terrain.

GeoShield Mapping Services focuses on landslide and soil erosion mapping, historical change analysis, susceptibility assessment, and monitoring. Our goal is to transform spatial data into clear, useful information for planning and decision-making.

OUR APPROACH

QGIS-based analysis • Spatial data • Cartographic design • Evidence-based assessment

Susceptibility outputs are not exact predictions of when a landslide will occur and should be interpreted alongside appropriate engineering, geological, field, and official hazard information.
